## Full description
🥠 **Crackpot Fortune** is a whimsical little oracle with real numbers under the hood.

Tap the cookie. It cracks. A paper slip unfurls with your numbers — and a fortune that tells the *story* of the pick.

**How it works**
Crackpot Fortune reads real draw history for Ohio Pick 3, Pick 4, Pick 5, Mega Millions, and Powerball, then studies the patterns players love to chase:

- ✨ **Destiny Blend** — a mix of hot numbers and long-overdue sleepers
- 🔥 **Hot Streak** — the numbers drawn most often lately
- 🌙 **Long Sleep** — the numbers that haven't appeared in the longest time

Choose your window — the last 30 draws, 100 draws, or the cookie's whole memory — and crack.

**Inside the jar**
- A cookie jar to save your favorite slips
- A fortune with every pick, generated from the actual pattern behind it ("The 7 has slept for 43 draws. Even dragons wake.")
- The current moon phase, because atmosphere matters

**The honest print**
Every lottery draw is independent, and no app — however charming — can predict one. Crackpot Fortune is for fun: it finds patterns because patterns are delightful, not because they're destiny. It sells nothing, takes no wagers, and awards no prizes. Play the lottery responsibly, and never with money you need.
