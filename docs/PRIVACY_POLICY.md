# Crackpot Fortune — Privacy Policy

*Effective: October 22, 2026*

Crackpot Fortune is a single-player entertainment app. It was built to respect a simple rule: **your data never leaves your phone.**

## What we collect
Nothing. Crackpot Fortune collects no personal information, creates no accounts, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

## What the app stores on your device
Slips you choose to save in the Cookie Jar are stored locally on your device only (in the app's local storage). They are never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Network use
The app works fully offline. Lottery draw history is bundled with the app at build time. The app makes no network requests during normal use.

## Gambling
Crackpot Fortune does not sell lottery tickets, accept wagers, or award prizes. It is an entertainment tool that suggests numbers based on publicly available historical draw data. Lottery draws are random and independent; this app cannot and does not claim to predict outcomes.

## Children
The app is intended for adults of legal lottery-playing age in their jurisdiction.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
